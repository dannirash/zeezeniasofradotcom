Heritage & Sourcing

Rooted in Sourcing, Grown in Gainesville

Zeezenia is founded on a simple promise: bringing authentic Mediterranean flavours and warm family hospitality straight to your table.

Family-Run Hospitality

At Zeezenia, Fawzy and Zaineb treat every customer like an honored guest in their own home. Whether you are a student stopping in for coffee or a resident shopping for the weekend, you will find personal warmth and genuine Mediterranean hospitality.

Fawzy & Zaineb's Promise

Uncompromising Quality

We are dedicated to sourcing only the finest goods. From our strictly 100% Halal certified meats prepared fresh by our butcher, to exotic spices, specialty sweets, and olive oils imported directly from the heart of the Levant.

100% Halal & Sourcing

Zaineb's Expansion

Under Zaineb's visionary leadership, Zeezenia has blossomed. She designed our supermarket expansion to include a tranquil seating area, a family-friendly recreation zone, and a weekend farm-to-table cafe that bridges regional traditions with Gainesville life.

A Weekend Destination
New Horizons

Experience Zeezenia's New Spaces

We expanded our traditional market into a lively agricultural escape. Explore our newly redesigned zones crafted for relaxation, community gathering, and fresh eating.

Authentic Hospitality

The Outdoor Bedouin Lounge & Study Retreat

Step away from crowded campus libraries. Zaineb designed our custom Bedouin lounge using authentic Middle Eastern furnishings and warm, rustic textures. It's an outdoor sanctuary created for students, local remote workers, and friends.

  • Perfect workspace with high-speed customer Wi-Fi & outdoor power hubs.
  • Fresh mint tea pots, authentic Turkish cardamon coffee, and warm baklawa served.
  • Comfortable outdoor seating shaded by beautiful green foliage.
Sustainable Agriculture

Our Potted Blueberry Cultivation Field

We bring the farm straight to your table. The new agricultural footprint features a precision potted blueberry field where we raise sweet, nutritious blueberries. Walk our beautiful farming lanes and enjoy seasonal community picking events.

  • Innovative potted crop system optimized for water conservation and soil purity.
  • Fresh organic blueberries incorporated directly into our cafe bakery items.
  • Educational weekend walkthroughs for local school kids and university groups.
Check Seasonal Harvest Times
Family & Play

Family-Friendly Outdoor Basketball Court

We want Zeezenia to be a true community gathering spot where youth, families, and neighbors meet up. Our brand new outdoor half-court basketball area is free and accessible for customers, creating a lively weekend playground.

  • Freshly painted court open during standard market operating hours.
  • Seating benches available for parents to relax while kids play.
  • Grab a refreshing drink or smoothie at the cafe and watch the action.

The Pillars of Zeezenia

Quality You Can Taste

Behind our farm and seating lounge lies the high quality Middle Eastern grocery core Gainesville has trusted for over 14 years.

In-House Halal Butcher

Premium grade, 100% hand-slaughtered Halal meats. From pasture-raised lamb, goat, and beef, to house-spiced chicken shawarma. Prepared daily in a pristine facility by professional butchers to match strict dietary requirements.

Lamb, Beef, Chicken, Goat

Fresh Bakery & Baklawa

Indulge in our famous signature Pistachio Baklawa—layers of paper-thin filo dough, pure sweet butter, and rich roasted premium pistachios, drizzled with homemade orange blossom syrup. Baked fresh daily alongside pitas and traditional pastries.

Pistachio Baklawa & Flatbreads

Levant & Spice Imports

Find premium Middle Eastern and Mediterranean imports. Fill your pantry with high-quality olive oils, tangy sumac, traditional za'atar blends, tahini, pomegranate molasses, and authentic regional Turkish and cardamom coffee grains.
